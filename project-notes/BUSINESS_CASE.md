## 1. The idea

- **Customer:** Who exactly is it for? My idea was a webpage that is specifically used by STFX students. This webpage is used to help students study at Stfx. It also allows other students to add previous courses that they took to help other students that are currently studying for it.
- **Problem:** What problem do they have today, and how do they solve it now?The problem that most of us have when studying is that google docs or the PowerPoint slides that were given by the instructor to study, are sometimes unorganized and you are unsure on where to start. 
- **Solution:** What does your product do differently or better?That’s why I created STFX StudyHub. It is a well-organized webpage that helps students understand what to expected on the midterms and final exam. It also shows students which questions are high yield, core concepts questions or calculations. This webpage also allows students to track their progress throughout studying so that they can know their readiness before walking into a midterm or final. The notes that students post in the webpage will also be reviewed before it becomes posted to be sure that it is accurate and up to date information.
- **Revenue:** How does the company make money? The company makes money from a monthly subscription, of around $9.99/month to keep it affordable for university students.

## 5. Cloud and operations

What would it take to run this for real? Which parts would you buy as cloud services (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS), and what are the risks of relying on them? To run this webpage for real StFX students, it would require more than just a basic file. We would need to use many different online services to be able to make sure that the webpage works properly for StFX students. First, we would need to buy student login and email verification through a service like AUTH0 or Clerk (SaaS). This needs to be done to make sure that only StFX students can access this and no one else can because then that could lead to false information it is also to make sure that student accounts are secure. Next, we would need to host the webpage on a platform using Vercel or Render (PaaS). Along with a cloud database like Supabase so that StFX students can save their information like their topics and tips and study progress, which will also allow them to see updates like the topics or tips that other students have added into the StFX StudyHub webpage. If a provider goes down or raises prices it could lead to 2 operational risks for example if a provider goes down right before midterms or finals hit and prices rises. To Solve this, we would need to set up an automatic database backup and portable code that allows us to easily move to another provider without having to lose the data so like the topics.
